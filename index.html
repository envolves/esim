Information

Frequently asked.

Is this a physical SIM?

No. This is a digital eSIM service. Your device must support eSIM technology before purchasing a package.

Will it work on a JV phone?

No. These eSIM packages are intended for compatible, factory-unlocked devices and are not designed for JV phones.

Are calls or SMS included?

No. The packages listed on this website provide mobile data only and do not include standard cellular calls or SMS.

How do I make payment?

Select your preferred package and continue to checkout. The payment screen will display the JazzCash / Raast merchant QR code and TILL ID.

What happens after payment?

After payment, the website creates an order number. Copy your order message and send it together with your payment screenshot to @esimsolutions.pk on Instagram.

What if I am unsure about compatibility?

Contact us on Instagram before making payment and provide your exact phone model so compatibility can be checked first.
